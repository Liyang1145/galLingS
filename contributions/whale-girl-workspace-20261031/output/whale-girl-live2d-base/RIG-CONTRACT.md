# Whale girl: native model acceptance

Use the blue whale character from the supplied references. Keep her full body, white cover-up, whale swimsuit, sandals, fin ears, ribbons, loop ahoge and one anatomically attached whale tail.

Production art is generated with built-in image_gen. assemble-source.mjs performs component size/position registration and pack-psd.mjs writes and roundtrip-checks the layered PSD. Original generated layer PNGs remain available. Any painted correction uses image_gen.

Required deliverables: editable layered PSD, native Cubism .cmo3, actual Cubism-exported .moc3, .model3.json and texture atlas; idle/talk animation or documented parameter control; a runnable local preview and runtime evidence.

Required behavior: independent natural blinking, speaking mouth, breathing with stable feet, small connected head movement, hair and tail movement. Keep all motion ranges modest until their endpoints pass visual checks. Do not claim 3D head-turn coverage or physics acceptance from a neutral screenshot.

Assembly issues to check: shirt collar must not cross the front of the neck; sleeves must hide arm roots; face/nose/eyes/eyebrows/mouth must align; head movement must not separate chin and neck; no duplicate ahoge; tail root remains behind pelvis; texture fringes must be checked on light and dark backgrounds.

Completion requires observed native binding and runtime loading of this exact exported model. Importing a reference layer or creating a list of parameter names alone does not prove binding.
